# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

> **Purpose:** Establish the business need, outcomes, scope, and acceptance measures that authorize and guide a change. This document states **what** the business needs and **why**; record solution architecture in the HLD and implementation detail in the LLD.
>
> **How to use:** Replace bracketed prompts, assign stable IDs, and link supporting evidence. Use **N/A** with a reason where a section does not apply. Keep the document proportionate to the decision; do not invent targets or approvals.

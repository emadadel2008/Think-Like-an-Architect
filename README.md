# 🧠 Think Like an Architect — Course

Welcome to **Think Like an Architect**, a provider-agnostic learning journey designed to transform how you approach solution design, problem-solving, and architectural thinking — not just *what* to build, but *why* and *how* to make it scalable, secure, and maintainable.

---

## 🎯 Course Overview

This course helps you **think like a Solutions Architect** — beyond any specific cloud platform — focusing on principles, reasoning, and design trade-offs that apply to all technical environments.  
You’ll learn how to connect business objectives with technical design and make informed decisions that balance cost, performance, and reliability.

---

## 🧭 Who This Course Is For

- Aspiring **Solution Architects**, **Cloud Architects**, and **Technical Leads**  
- Software or Infrastructure Engineers seeking to **move into architectural roles**  
- IT Professionals wanting to **understand architecture reviews**, design trade-offs, and system documentation  
- Students or self-learners preparing for **cloud certifications** or **architecture interviews**

---

## 🚀 Learning Outcomes

### 🧩 Architectural Mindset
- Understand how architects think and make trade-offs under constraints  
- Evaluate business requirements and translate them into technical goals  
- Identify and reason about **functional** and **non-functional requirements (NFRs)**

### 🏗️ Design Principles
- Apply key design principles such as **modularity**, **resilience**, **scalability**, and **security by design**  
- Select and adapt **architecture patterns** and **anti-patterns** to different use cases  
- Create **data and integration architectures** that align with system goals

### 🧱 Practical Skills
- Design architecture diagrams (context, logical, component, data flow, deployment)  
- Create **Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)** and **Solution One-Pagers**  
- Evaluate cost, performance, and operational trade-offs  
- Apply **cloud-agnostic design patterns** across Azure, AWS, and GCP

### 📚 Communication & Leadership
- Communicate architecture effectively to both technical and business audiences  
- Document solutions in a consistent, review-ready format  
- Lead architecture discussions and reviews with confidence
